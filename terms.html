The one thing you may not do is redistribute the library itself: you can't repackage, resell, or publish the collection (or a substantial part of it) as a competing transitions library, motion pack, or animation kit. Use them in your products, not as a product of their own.

Templates and themes. With a Pro, Business, or Lifetime plan you may use the transitions inside templates, themes, starter kits, and UI kits that you sell or give away, as long as:

  • the template includes only the transitions it actually uses, not the library or a substantial part of it;
  • the transitions.dev comment in each transition's code is kept.

Buyers of your template may use those transitions as part of it. A motion or animation pack whose main selling point is the transitions is not allowed. If your use case goes beyond this, email jakubja@gmail.com.

Transitions Motion Agent plans

The Transitions Motion Agent scans code for motion issues and fixes them. What each plan licenses:

  • Free: unlimited scans, and a monthly allowance of hosted fixes, for personal and evaluation use.
  • Pro and Business: full recipe access. Business additionally licenses automated fixing in CI (for example GitHub Actions) through the hosted fix service only: the workflow where our service and our model keys do the fixing, metered by your plan.
  • Enterprise: additionally licenses running the Motion Agent toolchain in CI and other automation with your own model keys (your code then flows only to your model provider), plus org-wide use across teams and repositories.

Interactive use in your own editor or terminal with your own AI assistant is included in every paid plan. Using your own model keys for automated, unattended fixing at scale without an Enterprise license is outside the license.
